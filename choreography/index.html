The Process

How a Choreography Project Runs

  1. Step 1

    A brief: the occasion, the date, the songs and exactly who is performing.

  2. Step 2

    Structure and song selection, signed off before anything is taught.

  3. Step 3

    Choreography built around the real ability of the group.

  4. Step 4

    Rehearsals scheduled around the people involved — not the other way round.

  5. Step 5

    Combined runs once each part is secure.

  6. Step 6

    Final rehearsal close to the event, with notes for the day itself.

Choreography — Questions

Does Dancewala Studio provide wedding choreography in Gurugram?

Yes. Wedding and sangeet choreography are core services, alongside couple, family and group, event and corporate choreography.

Can you work with people who have never danced?

Yes. Routines are built around the least experienced performer so nobody is exposed on stage.

How much rehearsal time is needed?

It depends on the routine length and the number of performers. Share your event date when you enquire.

Do you choreograph for corporate events?

Yes. Corporate choreography covers offsites, annual days, launches and team events.
