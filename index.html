Welcome

Welcome to Dancewala Studio

Dancewala Studio is a dance studio in Sector 46, Gurugram where children, teenagers and adults learn to move with confidence.

Some people walk in for fitness. Some come because a wedding is three months away. Some are parents hoping their child will stand on a stage one day without freezing. The reason matters less than what happens next: a room, a mirror, a song, and a teacher who breaks the routine down until it makes sense.

Classes run in Bollywood, hip hop, contemporary and freestyle, alongside batches built for kids, adults and complete beginners. Outside of class hours, the studio choreographs weddings, sangeets, family performances and corporate shows.

You do not need to arrive good at this. You only need to arrive.

Why Dancewala

Why People Stay

Six things that shape how the studio teaches, rehearses and performs.

Learn with Confidence

Every routine is broken down into counts and phrases you can actually remember, so progress feels steady instead of overwhelming.

Beginner Friendly

No audition, no previous experience needed. Beginner batches start with posture, timing and basic steps before anything else.

Creative Choreography

Original choreography built around the song, the occasion and the people performing it — never a routine copied and pasted.

Performance Focus

Class work leads somewhere. Students get stage time through studio events and showcases, which changes how seriously they rehearse.

Kids & Adults

Separate batches for children and adults, so a seven-year-old and a working professional are never taught the same way.

A Supportive Studio

A room where getting a step wrong is part of learning it. People come back because the studio feels like theirs.
